# Research integrity and AI assistance

The work is human-led and AI-assisted. AI assisted code implementation, tests, experimental scaffolding, analysis, and paper writing. ChatGPT also explained author-review fields and suggested wording; that assistance was absent from the original pre-comparison structured ledger and is disclosed only at report level. Synthetic Reviewer tests are engineering checks, not human scientific evidence. The human author remains responsible for all claims and release decisions.
